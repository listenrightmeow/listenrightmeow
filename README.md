## Current Projects

I'm focusing on increasing my confidence and governance through AI-driven development. Moment (mmmnt) is home base to the next generation of product development tooling aimed at getting away from the vibe mentality. Know what you're building, govern the outputs, and establish traceability for every decision along the way. Not everybody builds for regulated spaces, but how you build shouldn't change regardless if you're aiming for a regulated space or not. Build responsibly, with full intent, and get through your next audits for free as a byproduct of your product development approach.
